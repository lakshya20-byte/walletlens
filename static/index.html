    Analysis

    Methodology and limits

    What was checked

    Active ERC-20 token approvals for the address, read from on-chain Approval events. Unlimited allowances are grouped by the spender. High risk is reserved for an unlimited approval to a plain wallet (no contract code).

    What was not checked

    NFT approvals, token reputation, known-malicious address lists, balances and transaction history. Unlimited approval to a contract is common with trading apps and is not proof of malice.
